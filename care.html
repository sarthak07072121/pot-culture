General rules

If you only remember three things

When in doubt, don't water

Far more houseplants are killed by overwatering than underwatering. If the top inch of soil is still damp, wait.

Yellow leaves usually mean too much water

It's the most common distress signal, and the instinct is to water more — do the opposite and let it dry out.

Check the pot has drainage

Roots that sit in water rot. If your pot has no drainage hole, water less and check the base isn't pooling.

Indirect light beats a dark corner or a hot windowsill

Most gifting plants want bright light without direct sun — a metre or two back from a window is usually right.
