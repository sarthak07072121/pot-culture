Questions

Before you order

Do you deliver, and where?

List the cities you deliver to and what it costs. If it's local delivery or pickup only, say so plainly — people would rather know now.

What if the plant arrives damaged?

State your real replacement policy. A clear promise here removes the main reason people hesitate to buy plants online.

Can you put our logo on the pots?

Yes — on the pot or the gift tag. Give your minimum quantity for branded pots and the lead time you need.

How many can you make for a corporate order?

Give a real range and lead time. Every gifting buyer asks this first, and a straight answer wins the enquiry.

I've never kept a plant alive. What should I get?

Name two or three genuinely hard-to-kill plants you stock. This is where you win customers who think they can't.
