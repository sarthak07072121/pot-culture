Questions

Before you order

Can I just buy one plant?

Yes — single exotic plants are sold individually, not only in bulk. Bulk is for corporate gifting; if you just want one plant for yourself, that's a normal order.

Do you sell pots on their own?

Yes. We keep pots ready in stock, and if you need a particular size, colour or finish we don't have, we make it to order — say so and give the lead time you need.

Do you deliver, and where?

We currently deliver to ___. Delivery costs ___ and takes ___.

What if the plant arrives damaged, or dies soon after?

Damaged on arrival, or dies within the first ___ days? Replaced free, no argument.

Won't a gifted plant just die and leave our logo on a dead plant?

That's the actual risk with living gifts, so for bulk and corporate orders we only offer hardy, low-maintenance species — not anything delicate. Every order ships with a printed care card and a link to our care guide, and you can message us afterwards if a recipient has a question.

I've never kept a plant alive. What should I get?

A ZZ plant, a snake plant, or pothos — all three tolerate low light, need watering roughly every one to two weeks, and are genuinely hard to kill by neglect.

Can you put our logo on the pots?

Yes — tell us your logo and where you'd like it (the pot, a tag, or the packaging) and we'll confirm how and what it costs.

How many can you make for a corporate order?

From a single desk gift to a full rollout — see quantities and dispatch times in the table above, or just tell us the number in the enquiry form.

Do you provide GST invoices, or work against a PO?

Mention it in your enquiry and we'll confirm GST invoicing, PO-based ordering, and payment terms as part of the quote.
