Questions

Before you ask

How long does it take?

Two weeks from our first call for most projects, as long as you can get us your text and images in the first few days.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, photos, and a rough idea of what you want to say. If you don't have text, we'll draft it and you approve it.

Do I own the site?

Yes, completely. The domain, hosting, and files are registered in your name and handed over at launch.

What does it cost to keep running?

A domain is roughly ₹900 a year and hosting is free for a site like this. We bill those at cost, with no markup.

Can you update it later?

Yes. Small changes are billed hourly, or you can take a care plan for ongoing updates.
