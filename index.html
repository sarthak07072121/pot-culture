What customers say

Questions

Before you order

Do you deliver, and where?

Add the cities you deliver to here, and what delivery costs. If you only do local delivery or pickup, say that plainly — people would rather know now.

What if the plant arrives damaged?

State your actual replacement policy. Plants get knocked about in transit, and a clear promise here removes the main reason people hesitate.

Can you put our logo on the pots?

Yes — on the pot itself or on the gift tag. Tell us your minimum quantity for branded pots and how much lead time you need.

How many can you make for a corporate order?

Give a real range and lead time. Every gifting buyer asks this first, and a straight answer wins the enquiry.

I've never kept a plant alive. What should I get?

Recommend two or three genuinely hard-to-kill plants you stock. This is where you sell to people who think they can't.

Are the pots food-safe or outdoor-safe?

Answer honestly about glaze, frost and drainage holes. Delete this question if it doesn't come up.
